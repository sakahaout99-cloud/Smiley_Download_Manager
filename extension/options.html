Smiley browser integration

Connect Chrome or Edge to Smiley Download Manager on your Windows PC.

1. Pair with the desktop app

In Smiley, open Options → Browser integration. Copy the pairing token and paste it here. The token stops random websites from queueing downloads on your PC.

2. Choose what Smiley handles

About videos and YouTube

Smiley captures direct media files — MP4, WebM, MP3, and similar links that the page exposes to the browser. YouTube and other streaming sites protect their streams with signatures, DASH manifests and DRM. Smiley does not bypass those protections, so YouTube playback sources are not guaranteed to work. Only download content you have the rights to.

How to load the extension

  1. In the Smiley app, choose Options → Browser integration → Open extension folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome, or edge://extensions in Edge.
  3. Enable Developer mode, then click Load unpacked and select that folder.
  4. Come back to this page, paste the token, and press Test connection.
